# Lore Iconography

Standalone icon/splash renders for canon concepts that aren't places or characters — deities,
artifacts, and faction portraits. Matches PART 3 ("DEITIES & ICONOGRAPHY") of
`docs/Master_KeyArt_Prompts.md` and the artifact/faction references in `docs/GDD.md` and
`docs/Canon_Notes.md`.

```
deities/     The Celestial Weavers (Solara & Umbrion) and their associated cosmology concepts
             (Elder Song, Prime Essence, Cosmic Cycle, Seven Planes).
artifacts/   The Nullstone, Aether Rings, Arksilver, Echo Stones.
factions/    The five playable-world factions — Noctari, Solari, Orc, Terran, Human — per the
             culture sub-anchors in docs/KAYOS_Meshy_Prompts.md. Not to be confused with the much
             larger exotic-race roster from the user's other KAYOS-multiverse projects (Vulpari,
             Ursari, Felari, etc.) — those are out of scope for this repo and were left untouched.
```

One image per concept currently; if multiple generations show up later, follow the
`_gen1`/`_gen2` convention documented in `art/environment_turnarounds/README.md`.
