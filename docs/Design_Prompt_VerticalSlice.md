# Claude Design Prompt — KAYOS: The Night of Silence
### Prototype Slice: The Academy Courtyard & Containment Wing Entrance

**Why this location:** It's the single densest, most representative slice of the whole game —
exterior + interior in one continuous space, Elorin's Part One world at its richest, every asset
category from the bible present at once (character, environment, props, UI), and it's the exact
P1 "vertical slice" zone we're building toward in the learning roadmap. If one scene has to prove
the whole game's look, this is the one.

Copy everything below the line into Claude Design as your brief.

---

Design a single, richly detailed prototype scene for **KAYOS: The Night of Silence**, a narrative
RPG in the visual lineage of *Fallout 1/2* and *Planescape: Torment*, built as top-down 2D pixel
art at **Zelda: Minish Cap fidelity** on a strict **32px grid**, native resolution **640×360**,
nearest-neighbour scaling (crisp pixels, zero blur, zero anti-aliasing on sprites).

**The scene:** the courtyard and containment-wing entrance of the **Academy of Astral Harmony**,
in **Starfall**, second-greatest city of the Noctari (night elves), under a permanent starlit
sky. This is where Elorin Voidweaver — our protagonist, a brilliant outsider scholar — is
recruited into the research project that will one day create the Nullstone. The scene should
read as beautiful, orderly, and quietly ominous: a place of genuine wonder that we, the audience,
already know is walking toward catastrophe.

**Palette (locked, do not deviate):** deep indigo, violet, and silver starlight — the Noctari
faction color language. Cool, nocturnal, elegant. Warm accent light only from lamps and glyphs,
never from a sky source.

**Build every layer of the scene, left to right, outside to in:**

1. **Exterior courtyard:** slender crystal-and-darkstone Academy towers rising in the background;
   an observatory dome; a moon-bridge connecting two wings; silver lamp-posts (lit, soft halo
   glow); a night-blooming garden with dark flora; a low fountain; stone benches; a notice board
   with hanging parchment notices; two or three Academy students in matching study robes with
   house-color sashes, mid-conversation or walking a path; one wandering cat or small night-bird
   for life.

2. **The threshold:** a grand but not gaudy entrance arch into the containment wing, warded —
   faint glyph inlay around the doorframe, two Void Warden guards flanking it in sealed dark
   plate with silver null-glyphs, halberds of black glass, faceless helms, standing at rest but
   clearly not decorative.

3. **Interior — containment hall entrance (visible through/past the threshold, or as the scene's
   second half):** warded stone floor with an inlaid glyph circle underfoot; containment rigs
   along the walls — crystal capacitor stacks, brass armature frames, glyph projectors, silver
   wire "cable-vines" connecting them; a research archmagister (Corel — exhausted, formal
   high-collared robes slightly unkempt, dark-ringed eyes) reviewing a floating scroll-schematic;
   an observation gallery visible behind rune-glass in the middle distance; floating lamp orbs
   providing the only warm light in the room; a long table bearing precise stacks of documents,
   an inkwell, a rolled schematic (the safeguard design — make it a recognizable hero object:
   three concentric ward-ring diagrams on the parchment).

4. **The protagonist:** place **Elorin Voidweaver** — Noctari woman, mid-life scholar-archmage,
   long dark hair, elegant layered academy robes with subtle void-black trim, calm and intensely
   focused bearing — standing at the threshold between courtyard and containment hall, caught in
   the exact moment of stepping from one world into the other. This composition IS the theme:
   she is always standing at that threshold.

5. **One supporting NPC for scale and life:** **Sera**, a Solari sun elf enchantress in
   structured gold-white work robes with a tool sash of enchanting instruments, crossing the
   courtyard toward the wing — her warmer gold palette should visibly stand out against the cool
   Noctari indigo, a small color cue that she's an outsider here too.

6. **UI overlay, composited onto the same image** so the mockup shows art and interface together:
   - A **dialogue box** at the bottom third: 9-slice panel, aged parchment field with indigo-and-
     gold border filigree and corner flourishes, containing a short sample line of dialogue in a
     clean pixel-friendly font.
   - Below it, **2–3 choice buttons** in the "normal" state — parchment field, gold edge.
   - A **portrait frame** (square, arcane-filigree corners) in the bottom-left showing Elorin's
     bust, matched to her sprite.
   - Top-left corner: a small **Mental Strain meter** — a vertical gauge styled like a cracking
     glass vial, shown at a low/calm fill (silver-blue).
   - Top-right corner: a minimal **attribute glyph** cluster (small icons only, no numbers needed
     for the mockup) hinting at the character sheet without opening it.

**Overall mood:** wonder with a held breath. Nothing in the frame should look evil — the entire
point of this story is that catastrophe is built by careful, well-meaning, brilliant people in
beautiful rooms. Lighting should be cool and starlit outside, warm and precise inside, with the
threshold arch as the visual and thematic seam between the two.

**Deliverable:** one cohesive full-scene composition at a resolution that reads clearly as pixel
art (no smoothing, no gradients beyond simple dithered pixel shading), with the UI elements
composited in place so it can be judged as a genuine "screenshot" of the game — not a piece of
concept art, a mockup of the real thing.
