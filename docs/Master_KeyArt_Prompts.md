# KAYOS: The Night of Silence — Master Key-Art Prompt List

Copy-ready prompts for **concept / promotional / loading-screen / key art** of the main characters and
main locations. This is the **painterly register** (like the VS-### story panels and UI-011 key art) —
*not* the engraved cartographic plate register (that's `KAYOS_*_Map_Prompt.md`) and *not* the in-game
low-poly 3D (that's `3D_Asset_Bible.md`). Use these to build a visual bible, marketing art, loading
screens, and modelling reference.

All prompts are grounded in GDD canon (characters §6, world §4, environments §10) and the map docs.
Where a name/fact isn't in canon it's marked *(invented — flag if wrong)*.

---

## HOUSE STYLE (prepend or assume for every prompt)

> Painterly dark-fantasy key art in the tradition of illuminated-manuscript illustration crossed with
> classical oil portraiture and Planescape-era concept art. Rich, deliberate, hand-painted feel; soft
> volumetric light; visible brushwork; deep shadow with jewel-toned accents. High detail, high dynamic
> range, cinematic. **No text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no UI.** Not pixel art. Not 3D
> render. Not flat vector.

**Master palette by culture** (see the 3D Asset Bible §0):
- **Noctari** (Elorin, Talindir, Starfall, Umbraveil): deep indigo-black, silver-white, cold star-blue,
  antique gold used sparingly. The void as *presence*, not absence.
- **Solari** (Astra'Thalas, the Tower, the festival): white marble, warm & antique gold, ivory, honey light.
- **Orc / industrial** (Grakkar, the Ashpile, the facility): iron-grey, rust, ash, ember-orange, coal-black.
- **Terran** (Durak, the undercroft): ochre, umber, granite, dim geomantic green-gold.

**Recurring visual motifs to thread through everything:** the **Aether Ring** (a glowing iris-ring in a
Ring-Bearer's eye); **the Song** (canonically the *Elder Song* of the twin gods **Solara**/light and
**Umbrion**/shadow — ambient gold light that fills things: star-lamps burn without flame because the Song
fills them); **the Nullstone** (a **void-touched-obsidian** crystal that *drinks* light); **the lights
going out district by district** (the Night of Silence — the *Song being silenced*, an expanding ring of
drained colour). The whole game is *gold light being switched off*, so let warmth be precious and its loss
be the horror. Solari art glows with Solara's Song; Noctari art holds Umbrion's void. (See `Canon_Notes.md`.)

**Formats:** character key art → 2:3 portrait or 3:4. Location splash / loading → 16:9. Title/key art
(UI-011) → 16:9 hero.

## PART 1 — MAIN CHARACTERS

### Elorin Voidweaver — *the creator who cannot be innocent* (CH-001, hero key art)
> A 2:3 portrait key art of Elorin Voidweaver: a Noctari night-elf scholar-archmage, low-born but
> formidable, in her forties — reserved, precise, watchful. Pale grey-violet skin, dark hair drawn back
> severely, one eye carrying a faint **void-black Aether Ring** that seems to absorb the light around it.
> Dark indigo void-scholar's robes with fine silver astronomical embroidery, a single restrained band of
> antique gold at the collar. She holds, half-shadowed, a small fragment of **void-touched obsidian** that drinks the
> light near her hand (the Nullstone's promise — a piece of the Void she means to bring into the heart of
> the Song, to halt an *essence decay* only she has seen). Three-quarter view, chest up, against a deep indigo-black
> ground scattered with faint silver constellation lines. Cold pin-point key light from above-left;
> everything else in rich shadow. Her expression: the exact moment a brilliant person realises what her
> work will be used for — controlled, dry, a little haunted. Painterly, jewel-toned, illuminated-manuscript
> oil-portrait register. No text.

### Elorin, in exile — *the fading* (CH-002)
> Same woman, decades older, on a distant grey coastline at dusk. Stooped, a travelling cloak over the
> ruined finery of her robes, hair silver now. She holds a child's soft-chalk map of a valley (worn to a
> rounded nub at one corner). She is not looking at the sea; she is looking at nothing, *feeling* something
> 540 years in her future. Wide, lonely composition, cold silver-blue, a single failing thread of gold on
> the horizon. Elegiac. Painterly. No text.

### Grakkar the Unbound — *the destroyer with two centuries of reasons* (CH-004, hero key art)
> A 2:3 portrait key art of Grakkar: a powerful adult orc, ash-grey skin, broad and scarred, tusked, in
> his prime — but the eyes are a strategist's, not a brute's, patient and terribly deliberate. A healed
> scar ringing his throat where an **iron collar** once sat. He wears a laborer's rough garb over hidden
> discipline; in one huge hand, incongruous and precious, a fragment of **elven script on a scrap of
> paper** which he is reading. Faint ember-orange furnace glow from below, cold ash-grey light from above.
> Iron, rust, and coal palette with one warm spark. His expression: a man rationing tenderness like water,
> holding a century of arithmetic behind a still face. Painterly, heavy, cinematic. No text.

### Grakkar, young (CH-003) & elder (CH-005)
> **Young:** the same orc as a lean youth in the slag-plain, iron collar still on, barefoot, ash on his
> skin, watching his masters' lips move as they read — the theft of literacy in a single image. Dawn-grey,
> a cruel clean light. **Elder:** grey, massive, weary, one enormous hand resting on a dark dais, about to
> turn a key; the last warmth in the world draining out of the frame behind him. Both painterly, no text.

### Talindir — *the witness who decides what history remembers* (CH-006 young / CH-007 aged)
> A diptych-style key art of Talindir the scribe across 540 years. **Left:** young, earnest, ink-stained,
> plain scribe's robes, holding a satchel, receiving a heavy indigo-bound ledger from unseen hands —
> hopeful, out of his depth. **Right:** ancient, hollowed, translucent-thin, alone by a great window over a
> night sea, the same ledger open on his knee, a quill stopped mid-word — deciding whether to write the
> truth. Cool Noctari indigo and silver, one candle's worth of gold. Tender, grave, painterly. No text.

### Supporting cast (portrait key art, 3:4 — one paragraph each)

- **Corel** (Noctari archmagister, CH-013): *decency worn thin by duty.* A tired administrator-mage in
  layered indigo robes, papers in hand, rubbing his eyes; a good man mid-compromise. Dim study, cold light.
- **Coil** (Noctari theorist, CH-009): *the mind that sees the math before the cost.* Young, anxious,
  bright-eyed, robes a touch too formal; chalk glyphs floating half-solved around him; oblivious to danger.
- **Vara** (human prodigy, CH-010): *twice as good, half as credited.* A plain, proud, exhausted human
  woman among elven finery she doesn't wear; brilliant work in her hands, her name nowhere on it.
- **Durak Ironthought** (Terran geomancer, CH-011): *mountain-deep caution.* Short, granite-broad,
  stone-inlaid vestments, ochre and umber, geomantic glyphs glowing dim green-gold in his skin; arms folded.
- **Sera** (Solari enchantress, CH-008): *the outsider's outsider.* A sun-elf in warm gold amid cold
  Noctari indigo — she glows wrongly here; precise, guarded, loyalties divided.
- **Seravin Hollow-Water** (Noctari elder, CH-012): *kindness with edges.* Elorin's mentor in Umbraveil
  twilight robes, lit by warm amber lantern-river light; soft memory-scene haze; a face you measure yourself against.
- **Morga Steelheart** (orc elder, CH-019): *the arithmetic made flesh.* A stout grey-braided orc woman,
  steady and warm-eyed with a hard jaw, mid-argument, one hand raised — the revolution's conscience.
- **Kess** (young orc courier, CH-020): *a smile over fear.* Small, quick, light rags and a courier's
  satchel, a grin that doesn't reach the eyes; a spark of warmth in a grey facility.
- **Overseer Ilvane** (elven officer, CH-023): *the sincere instrument.* Crisp facility uniform, ward-baton,
  unsettlingly calm and courteous; the most dangerous kind of true believer. Cold, clean, symmetrical light.
- **The Archivist of Astral Wisdom** (CH-025): *the keeper who never asks what he keeps.* An impossibly
  ancient elf, half furniture, robed in dust and silver, surrounded by infinite scroll-stacks.
- **Grand Archmage Sulvaine** (Solari, CH-028, *invented name — Cold Open*): the aged celebrant atop the
  Tower at the ceremony's apex, gold ceremonial regalia, arms rising to draw the Song up — seen small,
  silhouetted against the lit spire, the instant before everything goes dark.

## PART 2 — MAIN LOCATIONS (splash / loading / promo, 16:9)

### Starfall & the Academy of Astral Harmony — *the ring city on the star-lake* (EN-001/004)
> A sweeping 16:9 establishing key art of Starfall: a Noctari city built inside a perfect circular
> volcanic caldera high above an endless sea of cloud. Concentric terraced rings of dark basalt step down
> toward a still black lake at the centre — and the lake **is not water, it is sky**: a mirror-perfect disc
> of stars, as if the world has a hole in it. Nine domed observatory towers crown the rim (one dark and
> dead, struck through). A single narrow causeway of stone, no railings, crosses the star-lake to a small
> island of slender towers and a great observatory dome at the dead centre — the Academy, ringed in faint
> gold, a moon-bridge arcing between two wings. Cold silver-white and indigo-black, antique gold only on
> the Academy and the sigils; rings of fine warm window-light in the lower terraces. Twilight, scholarly,
> beautiful and chilly. Painterly, cinematic, high detail. No text.

### Astra'Thalas — the Solari capital & the festival balcony (EN-006, Cold Open)
> A 16:9 key art of Astra'Thalas on the night of the two-thousandth Luminarae: a golden Solari capital of
> white marble terraces and gilded domes, nine thousand warm star-lamps glowing without flame, garlands
> and sun-mosaics everywhere. In the foreground, a high marble balcony with a worn balustrade; a single
> aged scribe (Talindir) stands alone at the rail, small against the vast lit city below where a festival
> crowd moves like one animal breathing. Beyond and above, the **Tower of Celestial Harmony** rises,
> alight to its apex where a tiny figure raises its arms. Warm gold and ivory, deep indigo sky, silver
> stars. The mood: the most beautiful night in two thousand years, one heartbeat before the gold begins
> to drain out of it. Painterly, cinematic. No text.

### The Tower of Celestial Harmony & the Nullstone vault (EN-007/008)
> A 16:9 key art: the interior of a colossal Solari ceremonial tower — tiers of gilded galleries spiralling
> up toward a distant lit apex — cutting down to a cold arcane vault far beneath it. In the vault, a dark
> dais under a ring of towering **ward-pylons** (interlocking lattice geometry, faintly glowing glyph-floors),
> and at the centre a single stone of **void-touched obsidian** that **drinks the surrounding light**, a well of
> nothing in a room built of gold. Above warm and radiant, below cold and void; the vertical drop between
> them is the whole story. Painterly, awed, ominous. No text.

### Umbraveil — the valley of the two-hour sun (EN-009, memory)
> A dreamlike 2:3 key art of Umbraveil, the Noctari homeland, seen through decades of memory: a deep narrow
> mountain valley in permanent twilight blue-violet, terraced dwellings with **open door-less archways** cut
> into sheer walls, **rivers of warm amber light** flowing downhill through the city like water through a
> delta. High on the upper wall, a single hard wedge of pale gold — the two hours of direct sun a day —
> strikes ranked crystal light-farms; on the deep shaded valley floor, the grand halls and observatory
> domes of the nobility (here, the dark is the luxury). Soft-edged, warm-where-it's-rationed, aching with
> nostalgia — the city a girl carries and knows no longer exists. Painterly, hazy, tender. No text.

### The Ashpile — the orc birth-camp (EN-010)
> A bleak 16:9 key art of the Ashpile: an orc slave labor-camp on an endless grey slag-plain under a low
> ash-choked sky. Roll-call posts, iron collar-stations, rows of miserable riveted shacks, furnace-glow on
> the horizon, chains and coal and rust. Orc laborers move in grey lines. No colour but iron, ash, and one
> distant ember-orange furnace. The cruelty is in the *routine*, not spectacle — a place designed to grind
> patience out of people. In the middle distance, one young orc has stopped, and is watching a master's
> lips as he reads. Painterly, heavy, cold. No text.

### The Research Facility & the Archives of Astral Wisdom (EN-012/013)
> A 16:9 key art split between two worlds one wall apart: on the near side, orc labor floors — furnaces,
> ore-lifts, chain-hoists, riveted iron, ember light, exhausted workers; on the far side, glimpsed through
> an arcane ward-gate, the **Archives of Astral Wisdom** — a cathedral of silver scroll-stacks receding
> into indigo dark, an impossibly ancient robed Archivist among them, cold and quiet and forbidden. The
> contrast — industry and knowledge, kept apart by design — is the shot. Painterly, cinematic. No text.

### Black Crag — the last rebellion (EN-011, flashback)
> A 16:9 key art of Black Crag: a jagged black-rock height on the slag-plain at night, the Last Orc
> Rebellion breaking against elven power — but rendered as aftermath and shadow, not a battle scene (there
> is no combat): banners falling, figures scattering in firelight, a doomed defiance already lost, one
> figure slipping away into the dark carrying something more important than the fight. Ember and ash and
> blood-black. Tragic, muted, painterly. No text.

### Lunaris — Talindir's exile study (EN-014, Coda)
> An intimate 16:9 key art of a small stone study on a lonely moonlit coast: a single great window on a
> night sea, walls of document-chests and shelving, a worn desk, a great indigo ledger open under one
> candle. An ancient scribe sits with a stopped quill, deciding what history will be allowed to remember.
> Cold silver-blue night, one small warm pool of candlelight, deep quiet. The whole game's meaning
> compressed into one lamplit room. Painterly, elegiac. No text.

### TITLE / HERO KEY ART — *the Tower at the moment the lights die* (UI-011)
> The 16:9 title key art of KAYOS: The Night of Silence. The Tower of Celestial Harmony of Astra'Thalas,
> alight and gold at the apex of the great festival — caught at the exact instant the **Song stops**: the
> gold light is draining out of the city district by district in a visible expanding ring of colourless
> dark, the nine thousand star-lamps going out in sequence, the crowd below beginning to still. Two
> silhouettes implied across 540 years — an elf who built the silence and an orc who loosed it — though
> neither is literally shown. Warm gold collapsing into void-black and cold silver. Awe and dread in equal
> measure. Painterly, cinematic, poster-grade. Leave clean negative space at the top for the logo. No text.

## PART 3 — DEITIES & ICONOGRAPHY (canon — for promo, religious art, UI)

The gods of KAOS are real forces (see `Canon_Notes.md`). Icon/idol/stained-glass-style key art, mostly
symbolic rather than literal figures — useful for faction iconography, loading motifs, and the Cold Open's
festival dressing.

### The Celestial Weavers — Solara & Umbrion (the Song)
> A vertical diptych icon of the twin elven gods who sang reality into being. **Solara, Weaver of Light**
> — a radiant veiled figure of white and antique gold, haloed by the first stars, hands drawing golden
> threads of the **Elder Song**; ordered, warm, scholarly (the Solari's goddess, honoured at the
> Luminarae). **Umbrion, Weaver of Shadow** — her twin, a quiet indigo-and-silver figure half-dissolved
> into a field of dream-stars and void, hands cupping darkness like water; the Noctari's god of dream and
> the void (Elorin's alignment). Between them the Song runs as a ribbon of light and dark twined. Painterly
> illuminated-icon register, gold-leaf accents, symmetrical. No text.

### The other faiths (one symbolic icon each)
- **The Way of Silent Strength** (Orc): **Krogg** the Hammer-God (iron, unyielding, mountain-forging)
  opposed to **Varlok** the Serpent-Whisperer (shifting, deceptive) — an iron hammer and a coiling serpent
  locked in eternal struggle; rust, ember, black. *(Krogg is the god whose visions drive Grakkar.)*
- **The Stone Voice** (Terran): **Petrocore** the Earth-Shaper (bedrock heart, weeping molten Arksilver)
  and **Aetheria** the Crystal Weaver — a mountain cross-sectioned to a glowing crystal core; ochre,
  granite, geomantic green-gold.
- **Felari Spirit Dancing** (Beast): **Lyrion** the Moon-Prowler and **Mawen** the Memory-Keeper — a
  dancing feline silhouette dissolving into a field of ancestral dream-stars; indigo, moonlight, warm earth.
- **The Path of Adaptation** (Human): the **Architect of Variables** and the **Observer of Outcomes** — an
  unseen hand casting a die beside an all-seeing recording eye; muted, empirical, clockwork.

## USAGE NOTES
- **Text always comes out as gibberish** — generate label-free and set any type in Affinity (same note as
  the map prompts). These prompts already say "no text."
- **Keep one winner per subject as the style anchor** so the cast and the world read as one production.
- For **loading screens**, the location splashes double as loads; for **character select / sheet art**,
  the portrait prompts double as UI-002/007 source.
- Names marked *(invented)* (Sulvaine's name; a few visual calls) are mine — overrule freely; everything
  else is GDD canon.
