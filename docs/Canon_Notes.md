## Franchise structure (don't conflate)
- **KAOS/KAYOS: Pieces of Fate** — the franchise. Also a specific RPG set in **347 AR** (player is a
  "Gatherer" collecting essence fragments; storyline *Echoes of Etherfall* / the "Gathering of Broken
  Pieces" prophecy).
- **KAYOS: The Night of Silence** — *this game*: the founding-catastrophe **prequel** (Elorin builds the
  Nullstone; Grakkar activates it 300+ years later). We build this, not the 347 AR game.

## The Song (grounds the whole Cold Open)
Reality is the **Elder Song**, sung into being by the **Celestial Weavers** — twin elven gods:
- **Solara**, Weaver of Light — patron of the **Solari**, scholars, ordered time, the first stars. The
  golden capital **Astra'Thalas** and the **Luminarae** festival honour her; the Grand Archmage's
  **Celestial Invocation** draws the Song up through the Tower of Celestial Harmony.
- **Umbrion**, Weaver of Shadow — patron of the **Noctari**, dreams, the **void**, the Dreamweave.
  **Elorin (Noctari) is Umbrion-aligned** — which is exactly why she, of all people, can build a void
  artifact.

Magic = hearing/manipulating the Song's vibrations. **The Night of Silence = the Song silenced = a cosmic
discord** (canonically the fault of the **Discordant Chorus**, chaotic essence-beings). "The Song is
eternal" is literal theology, not a platitude.

## The five faiths (one line each)
- **Luminous Remnant** (Elven): the Elder Song; Solara (light) & Umbrion (shadow).
- **Way of Silent Strength** (Orc Dominion): **Krogg** (unyielding strength) vs **Varlok** (shifting
  weakness). Krogg "guided **Grakkar** through visions" to the Nullstone — his diegetic drive.
- **Stone Voice** (Terran): **Petrocore** (bedrock) & **Aetheria** (crystal resonance); **Arksilver** is
  Petrocore's hardened tears (the metal Aether Rings are made of).
- **Felari Spirit Dancing** (Beast Race): **Lyrion** & **Mawen**; reality is the Great Dream.
- **Path of Adaptation** (Human): the **Architect of Variables** & the **Observer of Outcomes**; the
  world as the Cosmic Experiment.

## The Nullstone (canon spec)
- Project codename **"Aethelburg."** Built of **void-touched obsidian** + **the heart of a dying star** +
  the **sacrificed magical potential of 100 volunteer mages**.
- **Motive (canon):** not merely containing wild-magic surges (the 1453 disaster / "Northreach's Tear")
  but halting **essence decay** — magic itself dying, "a terminal illness." Elorin's "dangerous cure":
  bring "a piece of the Void into the heart of the Song." She built in safeguards; Grakkar circumvented
  them with **modified echo stones**.
- **Echo Stone network:** smaller Nullstone fragments seeded across elven lands extend suppression; the
  Night is a *network* activation, not one stone firing in a vault.
- Aftermath: the activation backlash fused an elven city into the **Fields of Glass**; post-Night the
  Stone was moved to **Khaz-Toruk** (the Orc capital), where the expanding **Nullzone** now threatens it.

## Races (game uses a subset)
Canon has **7 elven subraces** (Solari/high, **Noctari/night**, Sylvari/wood, Aquari/sea, Pyrari/flame,
Crystali/gem, Whisperleaf/dusk); **Orcs** ("Bloodsworn"); **Terrans** ("Stonewrought" — Surface/Solterrans
& Deep/Abyssterrans); **Humans** ("Elyrians"); **Beast Races** (Felari, Ursari, Avari, Vulpari, Cervari,
Lutari, Canari) + gnomes/tieflings/etc. **The Night of Silence uses only Noctari, Solari, Orc, Terran,
and Human.**
