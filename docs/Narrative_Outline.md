## 1. Form & Canon Lock

**Form:** One novel in two mirrored parts, framed by a shared Prologue and a closing Epilogue, with three Crossing Points where the timelines intersect.
- **Prologue — The Same Night** (omniscient; the solstice catastrophe glimpsed)
- **Part One — The Architect** (Elorin Voidweaver; 10 chapters)
- **Part Two — The Unbound** (Grakkar; 8 chapters)
- **Epilogue — The Same Night (After)** (Talindir's journal)

**Cities & places (canon):** Umbraveil (Noctari, Elorin's home — the valley city of two-hour sun); Starfall (second-greatest Noctari city, home of the Academy of Astral Harmony); Astra'Thalas (Solari capital; Tower of Celestial Harmony; the Nullstone vault); Northreach (the Tear, 1453 AO); the Ashpile (Grakkar's birth-camp); Black Crag (the rebellion's final stand); the Sylvari territories; the Abyssterran/Terran deep caverns; Lunaris (where Talindir writes).

**Cast (canon):**
- *Elorin's world:* Elorin Voidweaver (Noctari archmage, the Architect); Seravin Hollow-Water (her mentor in Umbraveil); Corel (exhausted containment archmagister at the Academy); her Nullstone team — Sera (Solari structural enchantress), Coil (Noctari theorist, her former student), Vara (young human prodigy), Talindir (apprentice scribe, ~80); Durak Ironthought (Terran geomancer).
- *Grakkar's world:* Grakkar (the Unbound); Rak / Garrakan the Walker (his father); his mother (a Memory Keeper); Morga (the rebellion); Zula Keeneye (the network); Kess (Sylvari-territory runner; the woman he loves and loses).
- *The night:* the Grand Archmage of Astra'Thalas (falls mid-invocation).

**Magic & world canon:** the **Song** (the world's continuous ambient resonance — "the sustain of a vast bell"); Noctari bioluminescence and deep magical integration; the **Nullstone** (obsidian sphere — heart of a dying star, void-touched obsidian, balanced essence of the seven planes, binding energy donated by 100 volunteers); the **echo stones** (Grakkar's 147-stone amplification network exploiting the "seam" in Elorin's safeguards); the **Ethical Architecture** safeguards (7-archmage committee w/ subrace representation — later quietly cut to 5, reps removed); the **Luminarae** solstice ceremony; the recurring motif of the unnamed **"supporting personnel" / the 778**; eras: **Age of Order (AO) → Age of Chaos (AC)**, hinged on the Night of Silence.

**Dropped from the trilogy (now non-canon):** Selantha and the Vethara foothills (→ replaced by Umbraveil); the character **Vareth** (→ recast onto **Coil**, see §2); the three-book structure and the title "The Weight of Safety"; the trilogy's Academy date of 1440 AO and Nullstone date of ~1466 AO (→ superseded by the locked timeline in §3).

## 2. The Vareth question (decision)

The trilogy's strongest Academy scenes hang on **Vareth** — Elorin's first-year peer, the brilliant-but-reckless theorist whose full-scale threshold array foreshadows the catastrophe ("stable transfer of *what?*"). He doesn't exist in the canon novel.

**Recommendation:** fold Vareth's *function* onto **Coil**, who is already canon (Noctari theorist, her former student, the sharpest spatial intuition she's worked with). Coil becomes the peer she meets early at the Academy, whose recklessness she argues with for centuries, and who later joins the Nullstone team — giving us one deep, recurring relationship across Part One instead of two shallow ones. (Alternative: keep Vareth as a distinct early-Academy figure who dies/exits before the Making, and let Coil remain the later collaborator. I think that's roster bloat, but it's your call.)

> *Locked: folded onto Coil.*

## 3. Timeline to lock (the draft contradicts itself — needs your call)

The PDF has three internal date conflicts. We should settle a single arithmetic spine before writing:

1. **Elorin's age vs. her arrival.** Text: arrives at the Academy in **287 AO at age 300** (→ born ~13 BO) *and* is **"903 years old"** at the Nullstone's completion in **1456 AO** (→ born ~553 AO). Those are ~560 years apart. Pick one anchor.
2. **The epigraph.** Her "most dangerous thing…" line is dated **"c. 347 AO"** in the epigraph but written **"at the age of 347"** in Ch2 — and age-347 isn't year-347 under either birth date. The epigraph date needs to follow from whichever anchor we choose.
3. **The run-up to the Night.** Ch9 says **"two hundred and forty years passed after the [1789] rebellion,"** then dates the first echo stone to **1827 AO** — only ~38 years later. One of those is wrong.

**Two clean options:**

- **Option A — anchor early.** Born ~13 BO; Academy **287 AO** at 300; the journal line at age 347 = **334 AO** (fix epigraph to "c. 334 AO"); ~1,469 at the Nullstone's completion (drop/replace "903"); ~2,013 at the Night.
- **Option B — anchor on the apex (recommended).** Born ~553 AO; Academy **~853 AO** at 300; journal line at age 347 = **~900 AO** (epigraph "c. 900 AO"); the resonant **"903 at completion"** stays (1456 AO); ~1,447 at the Night. *(I lean B — the "903, at the moment her life's work is finished" beat is worth keeping, and it sits right at her arc's peak.)*

**Plus, regardless of A/B:**
- Fix Ch9: change "two hundred and forty years passed" → "nearly forty years," so the first echo stone (1827 AO) → 30 years of finding more (→ ~1857) → the solstice all fit *before* the Night.
- **The Night of Silence / AC convention.** Lock the catastrophe to the winter solstice that **ends the Age of Order**, with the Age of Chaos reckoned from it (Talindir writes "the first day of … 1 AC"). Recommend dating the solstice to **2000 AO** (consistent with the wider KAYOS canon), the new age beginning the next morning. Confirm whether you want the hinge labelled 0 AC or 1 AC.

> *Locked: Option B; 2000 AO solstice; Age of Chaos reckoned from it (Talindir's "1 AC" holds).*

## 4. Craft directives (apply throughout — all four approved)

1. **Chronicle → scene.** The draft narrates centuries in summary; the upgrade is to dramatize the load-bearing turns at full scene length. Targets flagged **[EXPAND]** below. Keep deliberate sweep only where the *patience itself* is the point (e.g., the long middle of Part One).
2. **Two distinct voices.** *Elorin* = abstraction, theory, the listening-ear for the Song, emotion controlled by being "put away." *Grakkar* = body-knowledge (load, pressure, the exact necessary force), the oral "beneath-meaning" of the Memory Keepers, "information must be controlled." Push these apart so the halves *feel* different, not just cover different ground.
3. **Earn the losses.** Give **Morga** and **Kess** more on-page presence (recurring beats, not single appearances) so they fuel Grakkar's forty years of patience.
4. **Harvest the trilogy.** Import its fully-drafted scenes where noted, recast to canon (Starfall not Selantha; Coil not Vareth).
